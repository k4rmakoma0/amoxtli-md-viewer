## Links

Hover over a link to see its full URL in the bottom-left corner. Right-click it for **Open in**, **Open With**, and **Copy Link**.

- A known site opens directly: [Apple](https://www.apple.com)
- An unfamiliar site shows a warning first: [Example](https://example.com)
- A link whose text names another site always shows a warning: [apple.com](https://example.com)
- An anchor scrolls within the document: [Back to the top](#welcome-to-amoxtli)
